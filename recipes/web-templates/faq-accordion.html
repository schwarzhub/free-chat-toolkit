Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Go to Settings → Security → Reset Password. You'll receive a confirmation email with a secure link valid for 24 hours.
Can I downgrade my plan?
Yes — you can downgrade at any time from your Billing page. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial?
Absolutely. Every new account gets 14 days of full Pro features, no credit card required.
How does billing work?
We bill monthly or annually (annual gets 20% off). Invoices are sent via email and available in your account dashboard.
